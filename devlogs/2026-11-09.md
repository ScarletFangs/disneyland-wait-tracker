# Devlog — 2026-11-09

## Project

Disneyland Wait Tracker — Redesigning Wait Times in Figma with Disney branding

## Progress

- Studied how the official Disneyland app and website look and turned it into a blue to violet direction for the app. The saturated first pass was softened into pastels, and the colored wait tiles stayed for fast scanning.
- Worked out where a park switch for California Adventure would go and picked a park dropdown in the title, so the By Land and By Name sort could stay where it was.
- Settled every Wait Times state through a round of questions. Rides with no posted wait show 5 min, attractions that are not rides show Open, refurbishment reads as Closed, and closed or down rides grey out their name and Lightning Lane time.
- Organized the Figma file into numbered iteration rounds that keep every option, with a Chosen label on each pick, the saturated first pass restored for comparison, and a notes card on the branding.
- No code changed. The redesign lives only in Figma until the other screens are designed.

## Reflection

This session ran on and off over several days, and it was the first time I used AI to do most of the busy work. I ran into a mental block thinking about how much it would take to both design and code this app on my own time. The tech industry also seems to be moving toward guiding AI through the easier work while the person focuses on system design and design in general. To get the ball rolling, I had the AI draw and organize my ideas in Figma so I could keep making progress. My part was deciding what the app should look like and refining each round. A lot of those calls came from what theme parks do. I have never seen Disneyland post 0 minutes for a wait. The lowest is always 5 minutes even when there is no line, and Haunted Mansion is posted at 13 minutes when there is no wait. That is why rides now default to 5 minutes and only attractions that are not rides say open. I kept the rejected boards in the file and marked which one I chose in each round. I wanted it to show that the design went through iterations and that I considered other options instead of generating it once. I did not look into the other options extensively, but it is a small showing of what was considered. The single notes card does not fully work yet, but it is better than leaving the boards blank, and it should hold the big ideas so people can follow my thought process. I still want to write the back end logic myself and possibly some of the front end, but for designing and iterating on the front end, AI makes the process faster.
